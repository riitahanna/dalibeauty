About Us

Born in Lebanon. Built on beauty.

The first Lebanese beauty brand

Dali is the very first Lebanese beauty brand: born, raised and proudly made in Lebanon. With more than 50 years of history, Dali is a heritage brand built on passion, craftsmanship and a deep love for beauty. Today that legacy is carried by the fourth generation of a family-owned company, where tradition and modernity meet every single day.

From the earliest days the mission has not changed: redefine beauty by creating exceptional, high-quality products that everyone can enjoy. Great makeup and skincare should never be a luxury, so Dali focuses on premium formulas at wallet-friendly prices, making beauty accessible without compromise.

Where tradition meets innovation

Innovation is at the heart of the brand. Inside Dali's own labs every product is imagined, created, tested and manufactured, from idea to finished formula, right in Lebanon. That is what keeps the brand agile and forward-thinking, constantly launching new creations that speak to the needs, trends and dreams of its community.

Every product is produced in a factory certified to ISO quality, ISO environmental and GMP standards, because quality always comes first.

A colorful little world

Proudly modern, cruelty-free and quality-driven, Dali is committed to raising the standards of Lebanese beauty while staying true to its roots. Dali is more than a brand: it is a colorful little world designed to inspire confidence, joy and self-expression in everyone who discovers it.
